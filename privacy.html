Who we are

Onazi’s Import is a wholesale and retail store at the back of Sharia Court, at the Block Industry, FCT Abuja, Nigeria. For questions about personal information handled in connection with this website or your enquiry, call +234 816 404 3196 or contact the store through WhatsApp.

When you browse

This is a product information and enquiry website. It does not offer customer accounts, online payment, a newsletter, advertising trackers or analytics. Fonts are served from this website; maps and social media widgets are not embedded.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address and request information to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

When you make an enquiry

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp draft in your browser using the collection, quantity and any item details you enter. The form does not submit those details to a website server or save them in local storage. If you open the draft, those details are included in a link to WhatsApp. You decide whether to send the message there.

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or in person, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your contact details, requested products and order information. Provide only what is needed for your enquiry. Do not enter card details, passwords or identity documents into the enquiry form.

How information is used

We use information you provide to answer enquiries, confirm products and prices, arrange agreed orders, and provide purchase support. Information needed to respond to a requested quote or fulfil an agreed order is processed for those purposes. Where legal record-keeping duties apply, relevant records may be retained to meet them. Where consent is required for a separate purpose, it must be requested separately; sending an enquiry does not sign you up for marketing.

Order information should be shared only as needed to fulfil arrangements agreed with you, for example with a delivery provider where delivery is requested. We do not sell enquiry information through this website.

Storage and external services

The website stores only your privacy-notice choice in your browser when you make that choice. See the cookie policy for the key, duration and controls.

Phone and WhatsApp enquiries are handled outside this website. Information shared there is also subject to your communications provider’s practices. WhatsApp and GitHub may process information outside Nigeria under their own service arrangements. Read WhatsApp’s privacy policy before choosing that service.

Enquiry and order records may need to be kept while handling your request, supporting a purchase or meeting applicable legal requirements. This website does not establish an automatic deletion schedule for records held by the store or external providers. Contact us to ask about a particular record or request deletion where applicable.

Your rights and contact

Depending on the circumstances and applicable Nigerian data protection law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. These rights are subject to applicable conditions and legal record-keeping duties. To make a request, contact the store using the number above. We may need to confirm that a request relates to your information.

You can also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Learn more about the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Updates to this notice will appear on this page with a revised date.
