05 — Your Questions

A little clarity before you shop.
Still need a hand? Talk to us

Can I buy just one item?

Yes. We serve both retail shoppers and wholesale buyers. Ask for the current price and available options for the item you want.

How do I get wholesale prices?

Send your product list and quantities through our enquiry form or call the store. Trade prices and minimum quantities depend on the product and available stock.

Are the collections a live stock list?

No. The collections introduce the types of products we sell. Confirm the exact item, brand, colour, size and price with our team before travelling or making payment.

Can you arrange delivery?

Contact the store with your location to check available arrangements. Any delivery cost and estimated timing must be agreed with the team before your order is confirmed.

What if there is a problem with my purchase?

Call 0816 404 3196 with your receipt or order details and a description of the issue. The team will discuss the next steps. Your applicable consumer rights are preserved; see our returns and product support terms.

Where can I visit the store?

Find us behind Sharia Court, at the Block Industry, FCT Abuja, Nigeria. Our listed hours are Monday–Saturday, 8:00–19:00; Sunday by appointment. Call ahead to confirm the location and opening times before your visit.
